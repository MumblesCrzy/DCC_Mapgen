# Floor 1 — The Blind Borough (medium)

## Summary

- **Countdown:** 4 days
- **Zones:** 4 neighborhoods, 1 borough
- **Bosses:** 4 neighborhood, 1 borough, 0 city
- **Stairwells:** 5 in boss chambers, 2 hidden
- **Standard mob levels:** 1–5 (distance scaling)
- **Bosses roam:** no — confined to chambers
- **Janitor mob:** Vampiric Newts (Level 2). Spawn in proportion to the local kill count. Big fights breed big swarms.
  - *Janitor mob. These come in bunches. Like bananas, if bananas hated you personally.*
- **Floor storyline:** A turf war between two mob types that spills across neighborhood borders. Crawlers are collateral.
- **Entry point:** A — The Burning Undercroft (NW)
- **Seed:** 477717

## Layout

Grid, north at top. `*` = entry, `+` = one hidden stairwell.

```
[A*  ] [B   ]
[C+  ] [D+  ]
```

The four neighborhoods form one borough. The borough boss chamber sits at the center, reachable from all four neighborhoods.

## Borough Bosses

### The Borough (neighborhoods A, B, C, D)

- **Borough Boss:** Fenwick the Landlord (Level 9)
  - Form: a hulking chinchilla who has clearly been eating the other chinchillas.
  - Chamber: a bone-white amphitheater ringed with viewing screens.
  - Mechanic: Can be talked to. Cannot be reasoned with. Only vulnerable from behind. Turns slowly.
  - Stairwell in chamber: yes

## Neighborhoods

### A. The Burning Undercroft

- **Location:** NW. Adjacent to B, C. Distance from entry: 0 (ENTRY).
- **Mob:** Weeping Chinchillas, Level 1–2
  - Follow a shrieking alpha; kill the alpha and the rest scatter.
  - *The Chinchillas of this neighborhood have formed a cooperative. The cooperative has one rule, and it is about you.*
- **Neighborhood Boss:** Clovis the Landlord (Level 4)
  - Form: a hulking chinchilla who has clearly been eating the other chinchillas.
  - Chamber: a circular pit with a retractable floor.
  - Mechanic: Fixates on the lowest-level crawler in the chamber.
  - Stairwell in chamber: yes
- **Loot theme:** Weapon scrap: broken blades, spear shafts, and arrowheads. Good for a crafter, dead weight for anyone else.
- **Safe rooms:** 1
- **Notable feature:** A room where the ceiling is the floor. Crawlers walk on it upside down.

### B. The Sunken Nursery

- **Location:** NE. Adjacent to A, D. Distance from entry: 1.
- **Mob:** Pickled Vultures, Level 2–4
  - Attack in coordinated packs and retreat to regroup when one falls.
  - *Imagine a pickled vulture that has been told, repeatedly and by everyone, that it is not enough. Now give it a weapon.*
- **Neighborhood Boss:** Chadwick the Ever-Hungry (Level 6)
  - Form: a 10-foot centipede-thing with vulture heads grafted along its length.
  - Chamber: a flooded cistern with knee-deep black water.
  - Mechanic: Screams on a timer; the scream stuns anyone not behind cover.
  - Stairwell in chamber: yes
- **Loot theme:** Textiles and vanity: cloaks, jewelry, cosmetics, and enchanted accessories of questionable taste.
- **Safe rooms:** 2
- **Notable feature:** A locked door that only opens for a party of four or more.

### C. The Painted Dormitories

- **Location:** SW. Adjacent to A, D. Distance from entry: 1.
- **Mob:** Sniveling Beetles, Level 2–4
  - Cast a low-tier fear effect on their first hit.
  - *Not the strongest thing on this floor. Not the smartest. Definitely the most committed.*
- **Neighborhood Boss:** Tallulah the Ever-Hungry (Level 6)
  - Form: a mechanical contraption piloted by an unknown number of beetles.
  - Chamber: a cathedral of stacked garbage.
  - Mechanic: Feeds on dropped items in the arena; a spilled inventory heals it.
  - Stairwell in chamber: yes
- **Loot theme:** Goblin engineering: coal, gunpowder, crude mechanical parts, and the occasional working gadget.
- **Safe rooms:** 3; tutorial guild hall present
- **Notable feature:** A shrine to a god the dungeon declines to name.
- **Hidden stairwell:** at the bottom of a drowned shaft; requires a breath-hold or a spell.

### D. The Burning Sanitarium

- **Location:** SE. Adjacent to B, C. Distance from entry: 2.
- **Mob:** Moldy Sloths, Level 4–5
  - Distracted by food. A dropped ration buys thirty seconds.
  - *Each of these sloths was once a promising young sloth. Then they discovered crawlers.*
- **Neighborhood Boss:** Chadwick the Perpetually Damp (Level 7)
  - Form: a hive-mind swarm that takes a humanoid shape only when it wants to talk to you.
  - Chamber: a rusted industrial press room, machinery still running.
  - Mechanic: Can be talked to. Cannot be reasoned with.
  - Stairwell in chamber: yes
- **Loot theme:** Butcher's bounty: hides, organs, teeth, and other crafting materials. Bring bags.
- **Safe rooms:** 2
- **Notable feature:** A collapsed safe room, door blown outward. Something got in.
- **Hidden stairwell:** behind a false wall at the back of a mob latrine.
