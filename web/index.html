            Add a group met at the table

            Formed from reads as "formed from a hen party on a zeppelin tour"; leave it blank if nobody knows. Leave Crawlers blank and the generator sizes the group, as it does any other; met on floor 3 or deeper, a count is dealt races and classes straight away. Standing "us" is the players' own group: the generator deals it nothing.

            Dungeon Leaderboard

              Clear the rank to take a crawler off the list; give a former one a rank to bring them back, multiplier remembered. Everyone below a new rank moves down one, and tenth is pushed off. Each floor generated adds one to the multiplier of everyone still on the list.

              Add a crawler

              Leave Group as none for a stranger, or for one of the party. A crawler from a group is one of its members, so pick their race from it; the class comes with them. One from the players' own group (standing "us") takes any race and class. Whoever holds the rank now, and everyone below, moves down one.
