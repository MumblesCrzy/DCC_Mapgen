# Floor 2 — The Rusting City (large)

## Summary

- **Countdown:** 6 days
- **Zones:** 16 neighborhoods, 4 boroughs, 1 city
- **Bosses:** 16 neighborhood, 4 borough, 1 city
- **Stairwells:** 21 in boss chambers, 4 hidden
- **Standard mob levels:** 2–8 (distance scaling)
- **Bosses roam:** yes
- **Janitor mob:** Zealous Kobolds (Level 3). Consume corpses; each corpse eaten raises them one level. Clear bodies or clear out.
  - *Janitor mob. Each of these kobolds was once a promising young kobold. Then they discovered crawlers.*
- **Floor storyline:** A missing-crawler mystery; notes and personal effects turn up in a trail across the map.
- **Entry point:** H — The Hollow Abattoir (Outer NE)
- **Seed:** 754709

## The Collapse

Days ago, every structure on Earth larger than a shed folded downward into the ground in perfect unison — the Collapse. Anyone caught inside a falling building died instantly; anyone caught outside, in a basement, or in the open woke to find the wreckage gone and a dungeon in its place.

## Layout

Grid, north at top. `*` = entry, `+` = one hidden stairwell.

```
[A+  ] [B   ] [C   ] [D+  ]
[E   ] [F   ] [G   ] [H*  ]
[I   ] [J   ] [K+  ] [L   ]
[M   ] [N+  ] [O   ] [P   ]
```

Boroughs are the four 2x2 quadrants (NW, NE, SW, SE). Each borough boss chamber sits at the center of its quadrant, reachable from all four of its neighborhoods. The city boss chamber sits at the seam where the four boroughs meet.

## City Boss

- **City Boss:** The Gilded Gremlin the Twice-Divorced (Level 16)
  - Form: a mechanical contraption piloted by an unknown number of gremlins.
  - Chamber: a flooded cistern with knee-deep black water.
  - Mechanic: Only vulnerable from behind. Turns slowly. Drops the chamber's lights at 25% health. Bring your own.
  - Stairwell in chamber: yes
