# Floor 1 — The Drowned City (large)

## Summary

- **Countdown:** 6 days
- **Zones:** 16 neighborhoods, 4 boroughs, 1 city
- **Bosses:** 16 neighborhood, 4 borough, 1 city
- **Stairwells:** 21 in boss chambers, 4 hidden
- **Standard mob levels:** 1–5 (distance scaling)
- **Bosses roam:** no — confined to chambers
- **Janitor mob:** Screaming Possums (Level 2). Drag corpses to a central pit. Follow the drag marks and you'll find the pit. Don't.
  - *Janitor mob. They are screaming. They are possums. They are exactly as bad as that sounds.*
- **Floor storyline:** A smuggling route for contraband running through the boroughs to a buyer at the center.
- **Entry point:** L — The Screaming Catacombs (Outer SE)
- **Seed:** 914613

## The Collapse

The System harvested Earth's buildings in the Collapse and replaced them with this dungeon. Everyone who wasn't standing in a doorway, a basement, or open ground when it happened is simply gone.

## Layout

Grid, north at top. `*` = entry, `+` = one hidden stairwell.

```
[A   ] [B   ] [C   ] [D+  ]
[E+  ] [F+  ] [G   ] [H   ]
[I   ] [J   ] [K   ] [L*  ]
[M+  ] [N   ] [O   ] [P   ]
```

Boroughs are the four 2x2 quadrants (NW, NE, SW, SE). Each borough boss chamber sits at the center of its quadrant, reachable from all four of its neighborhoods. The city boss chamber sits at the seam where the four boroughs meet.

## City Boss

- **City Boss:** The Gilded Bat the Brood Mother (Level 11)
  - Form: a gaunt, 32-foot giant wearing the pelts of bats like a shawl.
  - Chamber: a neon-lit rave arena with shifting walls.
  - Mechanic: Only vulnerable from behind. Turns slowly. Weak point is exposed only while it performs its charge attack.
  - Stairwell in chamber: yes

## Borough Bosses

### Borough NE (neighborhoods C, D, G, H)

- **Borough Boss:** The Weeping Owl the Toll Keeper (Level 8)
  - Form: an obese, waist-deep-in-garbage matriarch of the local owls.
  - Chamber: a mock courtroom, jury box full of the boss's kin.
  - Mechanic: Immune to damage while any of its minions are alive in the chamber. The arena floor shifts every 30 seconds, funneling crawlers toward it.
  - Stairwell in chamber: yes
